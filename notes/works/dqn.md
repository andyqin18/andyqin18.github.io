# DQN (Deep Q-Network)

*Mnih et al., 2013 · [arXiv:1312.5602](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.5602)*

## Summary
Learns to play Atari from raw pixels by approximating the Q-function with a
neural network. Stabilized with experience replay and a target network.

## Key ideas
- A neural approximation of [Q-learning](#q-learning).
- Experience replay breaks correlations between consecutive samples.

- [Q-Learning](#q-learning)
